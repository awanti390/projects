# 🍫 Chocolate Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

I created this **Chocolate Sales Dashboard** to analyze sales performance and understand what drives revenue and profit.

The dashboard brings important business metrics such as revenue, profit, orders, customers, product categories, countries, cities, store types, and monthly trends into one interactive view.

The main goal of this project was to turn raw sales data into meaningful insights that can help in making better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and profit performance
- Identify the best-performing product categories
- Compare profit across different countries
- Analyze revenue generated by different store types
- Track monthly revenue trends
- Compare profit across cocoa groups
- Identify cities with the highest number of orders
- Create an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Some of the key observations from the dashboard are:

- **Praline** generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
- **Canada** recorded the highest profit among the countries.
- **Airport stores** generated the highest revenue among the store types.
- Monthly revenue remained fairly consistent throughout the year, with some fluctuations.
- **Low and Medium Cocoa** groups contributed significantly more profit than the High Cocoa group.
- **Toronto** had the highest number of orders among the cities shown.
